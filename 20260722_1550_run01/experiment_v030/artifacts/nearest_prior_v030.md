## Novelty ceiling

The broad research area and BFCL auditing task are not novel. At most, v030 can contribute a compact, reproducible revision-grounded localization protocol and a deterministic typed-contract baseline. Delivery requires a real chronological replication and cannot be justified by Development alone.
