# Research Map

## Evidence-backed failure

P084 directly shows that expanding a thin BFCL menu with semantically related but intended-function-different tools reduces AST performance across nine tested agents and produces wrong-function, wrong-count, wrong-parameter, and hallucinated-parameter failures. The frozen v009 cross-encoder also makes 14 top-1 errors on the 200-row Development set.

The current public trajectory search exposed real-looking τ-bench traces, but the only adequately sized candidate source did not document the semantic origin of its reward/return fields. It therefore could not establish false terminal commit against environment success.

## Alternative kernels and disposition

- Atomic NLI scope verification was not executable in the locked Python 3.11 environment from complete, attributable model bytes. No lock mutation or interpreter fallback was authorized.
- Direct learned score fusion and menu-relative lexical scope reranking both reduced out-of-fold final top-1 accuracy.
- Nested selective fallback did not isolate corrections; its three retained changes were all regressions.

## Candidate Promotion Audit

No Candidate is promoted. The available computations either lack a trustworthy final outcome, cannot run under the authoritative environment without an unapproved dependency change, or worsen the measured final outcome. Creating an implementation or opening Confirmation would convert a failed feasibility screen into outcome-driven testing.
