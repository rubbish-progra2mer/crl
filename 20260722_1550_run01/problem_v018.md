# Research Problem

## Target

Find a scientifically distinct, locally executable reliability computation outside the closed tool-retrieval and tool-necessity routes, using real public bytes and an untouched Confirmation carrier.

## Failure families considered

- Unsupported or hallucinated function-call arguments.
- Multilingual parameter values that are semantically correct but operationally non-canonical.
- Constraint omissions at the natural-language-to-formal-specification boundary.
- Missing postconditions that are treated as successful verification.
- High false-negative rates in memory-poisoning detection.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not substitute translated or synthetic fixtures for a released human-verified benchmark.
- Do not mutate the shared Python lock or build a planning platform for a selection-stage idea.
- Do not relabel standard fail-closed or provenance guards as novel computation.
- Do not create any subagent before a complete frozen Review Packet.

## Outcome

No route simultaneously survived direct-prior screening and supplied a real runnable Development/untouched-Confirmation path. v018 closes before Candidate freeze.
