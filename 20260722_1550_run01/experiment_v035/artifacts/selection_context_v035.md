## Selected narrow computation

Generic pairwise judging, generic pointwise-first judging, criterion
decomposition, order swapping and confidence calibration are collided. The
surviving question is narrower:

> When two plausible tool actions share most content, does a deterministic
> minimal-difference projection, paired only with the history and implicated
> tool contracts, improve an order-symmetric frozen small judge over full
> pairwise, full pointwise and difference-without-evidence controls?

The method is training-free and uses the same frozen Qwen3-0.6B bytes as v034.
No v034 score is reused. The only candidate-specific operation is deterministic
action parsing, removal of shared fields, implicated-contract selection and
bidirectional A/B probability aggregation.
