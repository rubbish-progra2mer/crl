# Research Problem v029

Related-tool menus contain functions that look globally relevant while only one part of their schema supports the request. A monolithic score cannot reveal whether relevance depends on the operation description, the argument contract, or only one of them.

The bounded question is whether a deterministic dual-deletion necessity score improves top-1 gold-function membership and first-gold MRR over the identical full-schema cross-encoder and controls that use either deletion alone, their mean, or their maximum.

The setting remains compact-menu function selection. It does not evaluate argument values, complete multi-call recall, execution, stateful Agent behavior, large-registry retrieval or open-world tools.
