## Selected computation

The surviving question is deliberately narrow. A shared training-only latent
space is fitted without labels. Successful training trajectories alone then
fit a task-to-action ridge map with equal total weight per task. For an
evaluated trajectory, the absolute innovation between its action latent and
the task-conditioned predicted normal-action latent augments the direct
action-text detector.

This is scientifically different from same-task support comparison: it has no
same-task reference at inference and no successful trajectory from a held-out
task. It is also different from v031: it uses the full action/terminal surface
without command parsing or fixed roles.
