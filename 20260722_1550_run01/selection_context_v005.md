## Alternatives considered

- Merely changing v004's strict-positive rule to non-negative was rejected as an outcome-driven threshold edit.
- Dropping `gorilla-pytorch` was rejected as post-hoc source selection.
- Keeping a single donor while making only an aggregate Claim was rejected because it leaves donor-selection variance unmeasured.
- Three donors were selected as the minimum ensemble that exposes control spread without turning the experiment into a combinatorial retrieval benchmark.
