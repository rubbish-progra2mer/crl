        8008            [FOR CHILDREN OF AGE 18 OR OLDER...]
        18008           Is (NAME) in school and living with you, away at
                        school, or not in school? [IMPUTED]
                ____________________________________________________________
                User Note: There is a problem with the codes for V8008 in Section
                E of HRS Wave 1. The question asks for children of age 18 or older:
                "Is (NAME) in school and living with you, away at school, or not
                in school?" The confusion is that for children who were living
                away from home, R was asked "Is he/she still in school?" with
                responses of 1=Yes and 5=No. If a child was listed as a household
                resident, R was asked the above question. Thus, codes 3 and 5 are
                really "No, child is not in school," and codes 1 and 2
                are "Yes, child is in school."

                        1.      In school and living with R
                        2.      Away at school
                        3.      Not in school

                        8.      DK
                        9.      NA

                        0.      Inap, not a Section E Respondent; no
                                children; no children 18 years or older;
                                child is not 18 years or older
