            1991 HOUSEHOLD INCOME COMPONENTS
VAR #
_____

SSPEN       SUM OF R1 AND R2 SOCIAL SECURITY PENSIONS
            AMOUNTS AT N41B, N42A, AND N43A.
            -----------------------------------------


M_EARN      MALE JOB EARNINGS REPORTED AT N3A-D OR
            N5A-D OR N7A-D.
            -----------------------------------------


F_EARN      FEMALE JOB EARNINGS REPORTED AT N3A-D OR
            N5A-D OR N7A-D.
            -----------------------------------------


UNEMP_WC    UNEMPLOYMENT (N9, N11) AND WORKERS COMPENSATION
            (N14, N16) RECEIVED BY R1 & R2.
            -----------------------------------------------


VAPEN       R1 AND R2 COMBINED VA PENSION INCOME AT N20, N23.
            -----------------------------------------------


OTHPEN      PENSION INCOME REPORTED AT N39G.
            -----------------------------------------------


PENSION     SUM OF R1'S PENSION AND R2'S PENSION FROM N26A
            AND N27A AND 'OTHER' PENSION INCOME REPORTED
            AT N39G.
            -----------------------------------------------


ANNUITY     SUM OF R1'S AND R2'S ANNUITY INCOME FROM N26A
            AND N27A.
            -----------------------------------------------


PENANTOT    SUM OF R1'S AND R2'S PENSION AND ANNUITY INCOME
            FROM ALL SOURCES.
            -----------------------------------------------


M_PEN       MALE PENSIONS REPORTED AT N26A AND N27A.
            -----------------------------------------------


F_PEN       FEMALE PENSIONS REPORTED AT N26A AND N27A.
            -----------------------------------------------


SSI_WELF    COMBINING SSI INCOME WITH WELFARE INCOME AT
            N37A-B-C.
            -----------------------------------------------


CAPINC     CAPITAL INCOME.  INCLUDES INCOME FROM A BUSINESS
           (N39A), GROSS RENT (N39B+N40BB), INTEREST/DIVIDEND
           INCOME (N39C), AND TRUST/ROYALTY INCOME (N39D).
           -------------------------------------------------


DISABIL    DISABILITY INCOME FROM SOCIAL SECURITY (N42A,
           N43A, N44A), VETERANS BENEFITS (N20, N23),
           AND 'OTHER' DISABILITY (N39G) OF BOTH R1 AND R2
           -------------------------------------------------


OTH_R      OTHER INCOME OF R1/R2. INCLUDES ALIMONY/CHILD
           SUPPORT (N39E), INCOME FROM RELATIVES/FRIENDS
           (N39F), 'OTHER' SOCIAL SECURITY INCOME,
           'OTHER' VA INCOME, AND OTHER INCOME REPORTED
           AT N39G.
           --------------------------------------------


OTH_HH     INCOME OF OTHER HH MEMBERS. INCLUDES WAGE OR
           SALARY INCOME AT N56 PLUS THEIR INCOME FROM
           OTHER SOURCES AT N57.
           --------------------------------------------


HH91INC    TOTAL HOUSEHOLD INCOME FOR 1991.  SUM OF
           EARNINGS (M_EARN AND F_EARN), UNEMPLOYMENT
           AND WORKERS' COMPENSATION (UNEMP_WC), PENSIONS
           AND ANNUITIES (PENANTOT), SSI AND WELFARE
           (SSI_WELF), CAPITAL INCOME (CAPINC), DISABILITY
           INCOME (DISABIL), OTHER INCOME RECEIVED BY R1
           AND R2 (OTH_R), AND INCOME OF OTHER HOUSEHOLD
           MEMBERS (OTH_HH).
           --------------------------------------------
