                Now I'd like you to tell me how some things are alike.
                In what way are an orange and a banana alike?

                INTERVIEWER INSTRUCTIONS:

                A.   IF R REPLIES THAT THEY ARE BOTH FRUIT, SAY, "Good,"
                WRITE DOWN THE ANSWER, AND PROCEED TO THE NEXT ITEM.

                B.   IF R GIVES A CORRECT, BUT CONCRETE ANSWER, LIKE "YOU
                EAT THEM BOTH", WRITE IT DOWN BUT SAY, That's right, you do
                (eat them both). Also, they are both fruit." THEN GO ON TO
                THE NEXT ITEMS.

                C.   IF R FAILS TO RESPOND TO L5a OR GIVES AN INCORRECT
                ANSWER, SAY "They are both fruit; you eat them both." AND
                GO ON TO THE NEXT ITEMS.

                D.   DO NOT PROBE "DON'T KNOW" RESPONSES.

                GIVE NO FURTHER HELP ON SUBSEQUENT ITEMS. HOWEVER, IF A
                RESPONSE IS UNCLEAR OR AMBIGUOUS, YOU MAY SAY, "What do
                you mean?" OR "Tell me a little more." RECORD
                R'S ANSWER VERBATIM FOR EACH ITEM.

                DISCONTINUE:   AFTER 3 CONSECUTIVE DKS OR REFUSALS,
                END THIS MODULE.
