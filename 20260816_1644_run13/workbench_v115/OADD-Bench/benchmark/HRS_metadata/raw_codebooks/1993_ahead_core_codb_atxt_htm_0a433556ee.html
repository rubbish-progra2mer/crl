                 SECTION A. IDs AND DEMOGRAPHICS

HHID      [HH, RESP, HELP, PERSONS]     HOUSEHOLD IDENTIFIER
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
     Permanent 6 - digit ID number.  Uniquely identifies each household.
     Link variable for all files.
          HH Level (HH)                      6047
          RESPONDENT Level (RESP)            8222
          OTHER PERSON Level (PERSONS)      17424
          HELPER Level (HELP)                3160

PN        [RESP, HELP, PERSONS]    PERSON NUMBER
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
     Permanent 3 - digit ID number for each record in the data set.
          RESPONDENT Level   [RESP]     8222
          Identifies first and second respondent in the household.

                    5931      FIRST RESPONDENT
                              IN HOUSEHOLD...............010
                    2291      SECOND RESPONDENT
                              IN HOUSEHOLD...............020

          OTHER PERSON Level [PERSONS] 17424
          Identifies a) household members (including children or
          grandchildren) and their spouses other than the respondent and the
          respondent's spouse/partner within the household; and b) non-resident
          children and their families.  One PN (from 410 to 600) is
          assigned to each household member and his or her spouse/partner.
          One PN (from 120 to 390) is assigned to each non-resident child
          and his or her family.  (See Section D, page 14 for
          distributions).

          HELPER Level        [HELP]     3160


HHIDPN    [RESP, HELP, PERSONS]    HOUSEHOLD IDENTIFIER AND PERSON NUMBER
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
     Permanent 9 - digit ID number.  Uniquely identifies a person within a
     household. Link variable for all files.

          RESPONDENT Level (RESP)       8222
          OTHER PERSON Level (PERSONS) 17424
          HELPER Level (HELP)           3160

HN        [HELP]    HELPER NUMBER
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
          Permanent 3 - digit ID number.  Uniquely identifies a helper
          within a household.

          The helper number (HN) was created by combining the helper's
          person number (PN) with their relationship to the respondent
          (BRELATEP).  (See Appendix A for details.)


BSUBHH    [HH, RESP, HELP, PERSONS]     1993 SUBHOUSEHOLD IDENTIFIER
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

          a household as of a particular collection year.  At Wave One all
          households are coded 0 in BSUBHH.
