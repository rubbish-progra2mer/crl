                     SECTION H.  EXPECTATIONS
            (All Respondents.  No Questions for Proxy)

     might be.  When I ask a question I'd like for you to give me a number
     from 0 to 100, where "0" means that you think there is absolutely no
     chance , and "100" means that you think the event is absolutely sure to
     happen.
